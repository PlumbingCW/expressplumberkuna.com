Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call or email Kuna Express Plumbing.

Talk to a plumber(208) 679-8357Call Now

Information we receive

When you call (208) 679-8357 or email contact@expressplumberkuna.com, we receive your phone number, the details you give us about the problem and your service address. We have no web forms, so we do not collect information through this website beyond ordinary server logs.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell dispatch at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use what you tell us to schedule and perform plumbing work, to answer your questions and to improve how the line is run. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only when it is needed to do the job, such as with a permit office when work requires a permit, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site is static. If we add analytics later, we will describe them here.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@expressplumberkuna.com. We will respond as the law requires.
