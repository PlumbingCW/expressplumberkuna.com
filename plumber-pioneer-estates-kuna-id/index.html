Hard water and what it does

The City of Kuna's Consumer Confidence Report says southern Idaho water is hard from naturally occurring calcium. White crust on a showerhead, spotted glasses and a rumbling water heater are signs. A water softener installation is one option, and it starts with testing your tap.

If a problem starts at the street

In Pioneer Estates the water and sewer mains are operated by the City of Kuna. If the street is wet or several neighbors have a backup, call the city's water line at (208) 573-7676 or its sewer line at (208) 573-1511. If only your house is affected, the problem is probably on your side of the connection.
