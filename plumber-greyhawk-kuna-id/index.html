Calls that fit a mid-aged neighborhood

Homes that are several years old start to show wear in parts, not in the pipes themselves. We do not assume any pipe problem here.

  • Slow kitchen and bath drains. Soap, hair and grease build up with years of use. See drain cleaning.
  • Water heaters nearing the end of a typical life. A tank that is rusting at the base or rumbling needs a look. See water heater repair.
  • Dripping faucets and running toilets. Worn cartridges and flappers are cheap parts. See faucet repair and toilet repair.
  • Outdoor spigots. A freezing night can crack a bib that was left connected to a hose. Shut it off before the cold and check it in spring.

Hard water in Greyhawk

The City of Kuna's Consumer Confidence Report says southern Idaho water is hard from naturally occurring calcium. Over several years that deposits scale inside tank heaters and on faucet screens. A water softener installation can help, and a quick test of your tap water comes first.

Reaching the city for street problems

The City of Kuna runs the public water and sewer here. For water in the street or a backup that affects several homes, call the water line at (208) 573-7676 or the sewer line at (208) 573-1511. A problem only inside your house is a call to us.
