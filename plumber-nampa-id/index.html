What counts as a plumbing emergency in Nampa

Water that will not stop, sewage in the house, or no hot water in cold weather all deserve a call right away.

  • A pipe has burst or a fitting is spraying water.
  • A toilet, tub or floor drain is backing up with sewage.
  • A water heater is leaking and the floor is wet.
  • The meter keeps running with every tap closed.
  • A water line in the yard has failed and the ground is soaking.

Shut off the main water valve first, then call. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 and your gas utility before anything else.

Permits in Nampa for plumbing work

The City of Nampa Building Department handles plumbing permits at (208) 468-5435. Per the city, most plumbing piping and drainage work needs a permit, but repairs to leaks, replacing broken fixtures and like emergency repairs do not.

Permitted work must be done by a state licensed journeyman plumber employed by an Idaho licensed plumbing contractor, with exceptions for homeowners who own and will live in the dwelling. Nampa says the 2017 Idaho State Plumbing Code, which is based on the 2015 UPC, is the adopted code.

Ask the Building Department about your job. Digging for a sewer or water service line has its own certification rules.
