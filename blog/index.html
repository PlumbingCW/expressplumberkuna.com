Plumbing Guides for Kuna Homeowners

Practical answers to the plumbing questions Kuna homeowners ask, with local notes where the City of Kuna publishes them.

Talk to a plumber(208) 679-8357Call Now

These guides explain what a plumbing problem usually means, what you can safely try, and when to stop and call. Each one uses facts the City of Kuna or another public agency publishes, and each says plainly when a plumber needs to look. If water is spreading, close the main valve and call (208) 679-8357 before you read on.

How to use these guides

Start with the symptom, not the fix. A toilet that will not flush, a faucet that drips and a meter that keeps turning each have a short checklist and a point where the next step needs a licensed plumber. The guides mark that point so you do not have to guess.

Local notes appear in a boxed section on each post. They come from the City of Kuna, Central District Health or similar public sources, and they say what the fact means for your pipes.

Topics covered

  • Hot water: expansion tanks, gas water heater replacement and tankless installation.
  • Drains and toilets: clogged toilets, drain cleaning myths and toilet replacement.
  • Faucets and fixtures: bathroom faucet repair and leaky shower faucets.
  • Water quality and pipe: iron in well water, PEX pipe and sprinkler backflow.
  • Cold weather and hidden leaks: frozen pipes, sewer camera inspection and a running water meter.

When a guide is not enough

Some problems should not wait for a checklist. A burst pipe, a sewage backup, a gas smell or water near the electrical panel all need a call first. For gas, leave the building and call 911 and Intermountain Gas. For everything else, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.
