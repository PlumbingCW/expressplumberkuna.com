Is Valor in Kuna?

Valor has a Kuna, Idaho mailing address on builder and listing pages, and it is described as being in Ada County. A City of Kuna council agenda from January 2, 2019 shows a request from M3 to annex about 990 acres at the northeast corner of Cloverdale and Kuna Roads into the city. Later city actions on plats in the area point the same way.

The annexation outcome is the part we cannot fully prove from a single document. At least one older news report described the area as just outside of Kuna. Parts of the surrounding land may also be in the county. So please call with your exact address and we will confirm service before booking.

Plumbing for a new master plan home

Newly built homes rarely have an age-related pipe problem. When owners call, it is usually about the first years of use or about a part that came with the house.

  • Water heater questions. Learn the model and age from the data plate. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.
  • Water pressure and hard water. New homes in southern Idaho still get hard water. See water softener installation.
  • Leaks behind walls or under slabs. A water bill that rises with no change in use can mean a hidden leak. See leak detection.
  • Single-level 55+ homes. Owners who want fixtures that are easier to use can ask about faucet and toilet swaps. See faucet repair.
