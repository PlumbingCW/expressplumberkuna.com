What larger homes ask of a plumbing system

A bigger house is not a problem in itself. It does change what usually needs attention.

  • Hot water that takes a while. A long run from a heater to a far bathroom can mean waiting for hot water. A water heater replacement or a recirculation question starts with how the house is laid out.
  • More than one heater or a large tank. Households with several bathrooms may use a lot of hot water at once. See tankless water heater for how demand affects the choice.
  • Many fixtures. More toilets and faucets means more parts that can fail. See toilet repair and faucet repair.
  • Garbage disposals and kitchen drains. Busy kitchens put heavy use on the disposal. See garbage disposal repair.

Hard water in Silver Trail

Water here comes from the City of Kuna system. The city's Consumer Confidence Report says southern Idaho water is hard from naturally occurring calcium. In a house with several bathrooms the scale shows up in many places at once. A water softener installation begins with testing the water at your tap, not guessing.

When to call the city

The City of Kuna operates the public water and sewer systems. If water is surfacing in the street or several neighbors are backed up, call the city water line at (208) 573-7676 or the sewer line at (208) 573-1511. If only your house is affected, it is usually yours.
