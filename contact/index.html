What to tell dispatch

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What the water is doing and where it is coming from.
  • Whether the main shutoff is closed.
  • Whether anyone in the home depends on hot water or a working bathroom.

A few details let dispatch plan the visit and tell you what to do while you wait.
